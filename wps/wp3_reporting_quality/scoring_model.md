## 1. Disclosure Quality Score (DQS)

### Dimensions
1. Coverage — share of ESRS topical standards addressed (E1–E5, S1–S4, G1)
2. Granularity — qualitative vs. quantitative disclosure; presence of metrics/targets
3. Double Materiality — explicit documentation of impact + financial materiality
4. Forward-looking Orientation — presence of targets, pathways, scenario analysis
5. Assurance — external verification status and scope

## 2. Climate-Target Ambition Score (new, Stage 1)

Applied to each company's FY2024 climate target(s); primary coding instrument
for the mandatory-vs-voluntary comparison (H1–H3).

### Criteria
1. **Pace vs. 1.5°C pathway** — how does the stated reduction rate compare to
   a recognised sector 1.5°C pathway (SBTi / IEA / TPI; see
   `wp4_indicators/indicator_selection.md`)?
2. **Ambition vs. own trend** — is the target more ambitious than a simple
   extrapolation of the firm's own historical emissions trend, or a
   restatement of it?
3. **Value-chain coverage** — does the target cover Scope 3 emissions, or
   only Scopes 1–2?
4. **Interim milestones** — near-term interim targets present, or only a
   distant net-zero commitment?
5. **Credibility of measures** — reliance on offsets/compensation vs.
   concrete, named abatement measures.

### Scale
Provisional 0–3 per criterion, composite 0–15 (to be confirmed during pilot
coding; may be revised to reflect asymmetric weighting if criteria prove
unequally diagnostic of mandatory-vs-voluntary differences).

### Anchor Examples
(to be populated during pilot coding, once a comparable pilot batch of
mandatory + voluntary FY2024 reports is drawn — see
`wp1_research_design/sample_definition.md` Section A.2)

### Benchmark Sources
- SBTi sector pathways
- IEA sector pathways
- Transition Pathway Initiative (TPI)

Multiple benchmarks are scored where feasible and compared as a robustness
check, since sector pathways from different bodies can disagree (see
Limitations, `docs/research_design/research_design_by_claude.md` Section 11).
