Data Availability

The sample of 1,056 European companies was retrieved from the Sustainability Reporting Navigator (2026) on 26 July 2026. Company master data, net turnover, total assets and number of employees for 2024 and 2025 are taken from Worldscope via Datastream (LSEG Data & Analytics, 2026a); monetary values are converted to euros by Datastream at year-end exchange rates. ESG scores are LSEG ESG scores (LSEG Data & Analytics, 2026b). Industries follow the 49-industry classification of Fama and French (1997), as defined by French (no date). NFRD and CSRD transposition status refers to the companies’ countries and Directives 2014/95/EU and (EU) 2022/2464 (European Parliament and Council of the European Union, 2014; 2022). LSEG data are licensed and cannot be redistributed; SRN data may be used for non-commercial purposes only.
