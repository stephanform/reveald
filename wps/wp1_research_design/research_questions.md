# Research Questions & Hypotheses

Status: 2026-07-26. See `docs/research_design/research_design_by_claude.md`
(Sections 2–3) for full context and positioning against Donau et al. (2025).

## RQ1 (Ambition — Stage 1, primary deliverable)
Under the first mandatory wave of ESRS climate-target reporting (FY2024), are
targets more ambitious and more credible for companies reporting under legal
obligation than for companies reporting the same standardised target
structure voluntarily?

- H1: Mandatory reporters show a faster reduction pace relative to sector
  1.5°C pathways (SBTi/IEA/TPI) and a greater departure from their own
  historical trend than voluntary reporters.
- H2: Mandatory reporters more frequently cover Scope 3 emissions and include
  near-term interim milestones, rather than a distant net-zero commitment
  alone.
- H3: Mandatory reporters show more concrete, less offset-reliant target
  design than voluntary reporters.

## RQ2 (Dynamics)
To what extent has the transition from NFRD to CSRD/ESRS regulation improved
the quality and comprehensiveness of corporate sustainability reporting more
broadly (beyond climate targets)?

## RQ3 (Market Pricing — Stage 2, correlational)
Is higher disclosure quality / climate-target ambition associated with
differences in firms' market valuation and risk indicators?

- H4: Companies with higher DQS / Ambition scores show different market
  valuation and risk-indicator profiles, controlling for size, leverage, and
  sector. (Association, not causal — see Section 11 of the research design.)

## RQ4 (Comparative)
How do reporting quality and climate-target ambition vary across countries,
industries, and mandatory/voluntary regulatory exposure groups?

- H5: The effect of mandatory/voluntary status on ambition (H1–H3) is
  moderated by firm size, industry carbon intensity, and country-level
  implementation status.

## Outlook (future project, not in current scope)
Do firms subject to ESRS actually close in on their stated climate targets
over time? Requires ≥2026 reporting years; this project's Stage 1 dataset is
the starting point.
