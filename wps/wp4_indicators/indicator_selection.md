# Indicator Selection

Status: 2026-07-26.

## Sustainability Indicators
- Emissions intensity (Scope 1+2)
- Energy intensity
- Target pathway disclosure

## Climate-Target Ambition Benchmarks (Stage 1, new)

Free, publicly available sector decarbonisation pathways used to benchmark
stated climate targets against a 1.5°C trajectory (see
`wp3_reporting_quality/scoring_model.md`, Criterion 1):

- **Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)** — sector-specific 1.5°C
  pathways used as the primary benchmark
- **International Energy Agency (IEA)** — sector pathways (e.g. Net Zero
  Emissions by 2050 Scenario), used as a cross-check
- **Transition Pathway Initiative (TPI)** — sector benchmarks and management
  quality assessments, used as a second cross-check

Scored against multiple benchmarks where feasible, since pathways from
different bodies can disagree on required pace by sector — reported as a
robustness check rather than collapsed into a single number.
