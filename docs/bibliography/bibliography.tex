% ── Citation examples ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\section{Citation Examples}

\subsection{Parenthetical citation}
Estimates of the industry cost of equity are inherently imprecise
\citep{fama1997industry}.

\subsection{In-text citation}
\citet{fama1997industry} show that standard errors for cost of equity
estimates exceed 3\% per year, regardless of whether the CAPM or the
three-factor model is used.

\subsection{Year only}
The result was first established in \citeyear{fama1997industry} and has
since become a standard reference point in corporate finance.

\subsection{Author only}
\citeauthor{fama1997industry} argue that the imprecision stems from
both uncertain factor risk premiums and unstable factor loadings.
